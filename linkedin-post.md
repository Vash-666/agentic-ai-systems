Built something cool this week: a multi-agent AI system that runs like a well-oiled dev team instead of a single overworked engineer.

**What does that mean?**

Think of agentic AI as specialized team members. Instead of one AI doing everything, you have @switch (orchestrating everything), @quality (auditing work), @content (creating documentation), @product (prioritizing features), @scaffolder (setting up projects), and @grok (bridging external APIs) — each with a specific job. They coordinate, hand off work, and run checks just like a real team.

**Health monitoring** = your system dashboard. 14 of 15 checks passing means the infrastructure is solid. You know what's working and what's not before users do.

**Async spawning** = parallel processing. Instead of agents waiting in line, they work simultaneously. Faster execution, better resource use.

The result? 9.26/10 quality score on the latest build. Not hype — just solid engineering.
