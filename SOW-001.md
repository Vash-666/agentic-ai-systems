## 1. Executive Summary

This Scope of Work defines the deliverables, timeline, and requirements for the personal brand/legacy website for **Sant Gupta**. The website will tell the story of a life journey — from early childhood in India through IIT Delhi (1969), migration to the USA, a distinguished career in the defense industry, community leadership, and present-day mentorship.

**Positioning Statement:** *"From IIT Delhi '69 to a Life of Impact — Now Mentoring"*

---

## 2. Project Scope

### 2.1 Website Structure & Pages

Based on the "A Life in Snapshot" concept with 6 life chapters, we recommend a **hybrid approach** combining the narrative flow of a landing page with the depth of a multi-page site.

**Recommended Template:** `portfolio` (adapted for personal brand storytelling)

**Rationale:**
- Portfolio templates excel at visual storytelling and chronological progression
- Built-in gallery support for photos across life chapters
- Clean, distinguished aesthetic suitable for a legacy site
- Easy to adapt CTAs for mentorship rather than client work

#### Page Structure

| Page | Purpose | Key Content |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Home / Hero** | First impression, positioning | Headline, positioning statement, hero image, scroll indicator |
| **Chapter 1: Early Life** | Childhood origins | Family of 5, formative years in India |
| **Chapter 2: IIT Delhi** | Educational foundation | 1969 B.Tech EE, overcoming English challenge, sports, friendships |
| **Chapter 3: Journey to USA** | Migration story | Early 1970s migration, building family |
| **Chapter 4: Professional Career** | Career achievements | Defense industry VP, contributions, milestones |
| **Chapter 5: Community Contribution** | Giving back | Durga Temple President, USHA involvement |
| **Chapter 6: Present & Future** | Current chapter | 70s, mentorship, travel, giving back |
| **Connect / Contact** | CTAs & engagement | Mentorship form, alumni networking, community involvement |
| **Gallery** | Photo collection | Curated photos across all chapters |

### 2.2 Features & Functionality

#### Core Features
- [ ] Responsive design (mobile-first)
- [ ] Photo-forward layout with lazy loading
- [ ] Smooth scroll navigation between chapters
- [ ] Contact/mentorship inquiry form
- [ ] Social sharing (LinkedIn, Twitter/X)
- [ ] Print-friendly stylesheet for legacy preservation

#### Accessibility Requirements
- [ ] Minimum 18px base font size
- [ ] WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- [ ] Alt text for all images
- [ ] Keyboard navigation support
- [ ] Reduced motion support for animations

#### Optional Enhancements (V2)
- [ ] Video testimonials/embeds
- [ ] Interactive timeline
- [ ] Guestbook/messages from visitors
- [ ] Newsletter signup for mentorship updates

## 3. Design Requirements

### 3.1 Visual Identity

**Tone:** Warm & approachable, distinguished but accessible

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Warm earth tones (terracotta, sand, sage)
- Secondary: Deep navy or charcoal for contrast
- Accent: Gold/bronze highlights for distinguished feel
- Background: Warm off-white or cream (not stark white)

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Serif font (elegant, timeless)
- Body: Sans-serif (readable, accessible 18px+)
- Accent: Script or handwritten for personal quotes

**Imagery Style:**
- Photo-forward with generous white space
- Mix of archival photos and current portraits
- Subtle vignettes or frames for historical photos

### 3.2 Target Personas

1. **IIT Students/Alumni** — Seeking inspiration, mentorship, connection
2. **Young Professionals** — Looking for career guidance, immigrant success stories
3. **Community Peers** — Fellow temple members, USHA colleagues, friends

### 4.2 Performance Targets

- Lighthouse Performance Score: 90+
- First Contentful Paint: < 1.5s
- Time to Interactive: < 3.5s
- Image optimization: WebP with JPEG fallback

### 4.3 SEO Requirements

- Semantic HTML structure
- Meta descriptions for each chapter
- Open Graph tags for social sharing
- Structured data (Person schema)
- Sitemap.xml generation
