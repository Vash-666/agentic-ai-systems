## 3. Target User Personas

### Primary Audience: Elite Athletes & Coaches

**Persona: "Aspiring Champion Athlete"**
- **Demographics:** 18-30 years old, professional or Olympic-aspiring athletes
- **Goals:** Find a physio who understands high-performance demands; prevent injuries; extend career longevity
- **Pain Points:** Previous physios didn't understand sport-specific needs; short-term fixes that don't last; difficulty finding someone who travels/competes with them
- **Decision Factors:** Credibility (who have they worked with?), approach (do they get my sport?), availability (can they travel?)
- **Conversion Goal:** Book 1:1 consultation or assessment

**Persona: "Elite Coach / Sports Manager"**
- **Demographics:** 35-55 years old, coaches of national/international teams, sports management professionals
- **Goals:** Find reliable rehab partner for their athletes; ensure team health and performance
- **Pain Points:** Inconsistent physio quality; lack of communication; physios who don't integrate with training schedules
- **Decision Factors:** Track record, professional network, communication style, flexibility
- **Conversion Goal:** Partnership inquiry, team contract discussion

### Secondary Audience: Sports Physiotherapists & Rehab Professionals

**Persona: "Developing Sports Physio"**
- **Demographics:** 25-40 years old, early-to-mid career physiotherapists interested in sports specialization
- **Goals:** Learn from an established expert; understand elite-level protocols; advance their career
- **Pain Points:** Limited access to mentorship; lack of structured learning paths; difficulty breaking into elite sports
- **Decision Factors:** Educational offerings, mentorship availability, credibility of instructor
- **Conversion Goal:** Enroll in mentorship program, attend workshop/webinar

**Persona: "Established Practitioner Seeking Growth"**
- **Demographics:** 35-50 years old, experienced physios looking to specialize or expand
- **Goals:** Learn advanced techniques; understand business of elite sports physio; network
- **Pain Factors:** Plateaued career; want to work with higher-level athletes; seeking new revenue streams
- **Conversion Goal:** Advanced certification program, consulting engagement

### Tertiary Audience: Sports Federations & Brands

**Persona: "Federation Decision Maker"**
- **Demographics:** 40-60 years old, administrators at national/state sports federations
- **Goals:** Improve athlete support infrastructure; reduce injury rates; improve medal outcomes
- **Pain Points:** Budget constraints; need for measurable ROI; finding qualified professionals
- **Conversion Goal:** Consulting contract, advisory board position, speaking engagement

**Persona: "Sports Brand / Corporate Wellness"**
- **Demographics:** Marketing/HR leads at sports brands, corporate wellness companies
- **Goals:** Associate with credible experts; content partnerships; athlete wellness programs
- **Conversion Goal:** Brand partnership inquiry, content collaboration, speaking engagement

## 5. Key Conversion Points

### Primary CTAs (Above the Fold)

1. **"Work With Me"** - For athletes/coaches seeking 1:1 services
   - Destination: Contact form with service type selection
   - Priority: Highest

2. **"Explore My Approach"** - For researchers/learning-oriented visitors
   - Destination: Expertise & Approach page
   - Priority: High

### Secondary CTAs (Throughout Site)

3. **"Book a Consultation"** - Direct booking for qualified leads
   - Destination: Calendly/similar scheduling integration
   - Trigger: After demonstrating value (post-case study, post-methodology)

4. **"Join the Mentorship"** - For physio professionals
   - Destination: Program details + application form
   - Location: Offerings page, Insights section

5. **"Invite Me to Speak"** - For event organizers/brands
   - Destination: Speaking inquiry form
   - Location: Offerings page, Media section

6. **"Download the Guide"** - Lead magnet for email capture
   - Destination: Gated content (email required)
   - Location: Blog posts, footer

### Form Requirements

| Form Type | Fields Needed | Auto-Response |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| Athlete Inquiry | Name, sport, level (national/international/Olympic), injury/goal, contact, preferred contact method | Thank you + response time expectation (24-48h) |
| Speaking Request | Organization, event type, date, audience size, topic interest, budget range, contact | Thank you + availability check promise |
| Media Inquiry | Outlet, deadline, angle, contact, interview format | Thank you + direct email for urgent requests |
| Mentorship Interest | Name, credentials, experience level, goals, contact | Program details + application timeline |
| General Contact | Name, inquiry type, message, contact | Standard thank you |

### Lead Capture Strategy

- **Exit Intent:** Offer "Injury Prevention Checklist for [Sport]" based on page context
- **Blog Subscriptions:** Weekly insights newsletter signup
- **Resource Downloads:** Gated premium content (protocols, guides)
- **Webinar Registration:** Free monthly educational sessions

## 6. Technical Requirements

### Performance & Speed

- **Target Load Time:** < 2 seconds (First Contentful Paint)
- **Core Web Vitals:** All metrics in "Good" range
- **Mobile Performance:** 90+ Lighthouse score on mobile
- **Image Optimization:** WebP format, lazy loading, responsive images
- **CDN:** Required for global athlete audience

### Responsive Design

- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (320px), Tablet (768px), Desktop (1024px+), Large Desktop (1440px+)
- **Mobile-First:** Design prioritizes mobile experience (athletes often browse on phones)
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum 44px for all interactive elements
- **Typography:** Readable at all sizes (minimum 16px body text)

### SEO Requirements

- **Meta Tags:** Custom title/description for every page
- **Schema Markup:** Person, Article, Event, FAQ schemas
- **URL Structure:** Clean, descriptive URLs (/about, /expertise, /contact)
- **Sitemap:** Auto-generated XML sitemap
- **Robots.txt:** Properly configured
- **Canonical URLs:** Prevent duplicate content issues

### Key SEO Targets

| Keyword Target | Intent | Priority |
|----------------|--------|----------|
| "sports physiotherapist India" | Service discovery | High |
| "Olympic physiotherapist" | Credibility search | High |
| "athlete rehabilitation" | Problem-aware | Medium |
| "sports injury prevention" | Education | Medium |
| "Neeraj Chopra physiotherapist" | Branded search | High |
| "performance rehabilitation architect" | Positioning | Medium |

### Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

- Alt text for all images
- Keyboard navigation support
- ARIA labels for interactive elements
- Color contrast ratio 4.5:1 minimum
- Focus indicators visible
- Screen reader compatibility

### Security

- **SSL Certificate:** HTTPS required
- **Form Security:** CSRF protection, input validation
- **Privacy Compliance:** GDPR/CCPA ready (cookie consent, privacy policy)
- **Data Handling:** Secure storage of contact information

### Analytics & Tracking

- **Google Analytics 4:** Page views, events, conversions
- **Heatmaps:** Hotjar or similar for UX optimization
- **Form Analytics:** Track abandonment rates
- **UTM Parameters:** Track campaign effectiveness
- **Conversion Tracking:** Goal completions by source

## 7. Visual Design Direction

### Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| **Primary** | Deep Navy/Teal | #1A3A52 | Headers, primary text, trust elements |
| **Secondary** | Fresh Green | #2D8B6B | CTAs, highlights, growth symbolism |
| **Accent** | Subtle Gold | #C9A227 | Achievements, premium touches, Olympic connection |
| **Background** | Crisp White | #FFFFFF | Primary backgrounds, clean space |
| **Surface** | Light Gray | #F5F7F9 | Cards, sections, subtle separation |

### Typography

- **Headings:** Modern sans-serif (suggested: Inter, Montserrat, or similar)
  - H1: 48-64px (desktop), 32-40px (mobile)
  - H2: 36-48px (desktop), 24-32px (mobile)
  - H3: 24-32px (desktop), 20-24px (mobile)
  
- **Body:** Highly readable sans-serif (suggested: Inter, Open Sans)
  - Body: 16-18px, line-height 1.6-1.8
  - Small: 14px for captions, metadata

### Imagery Style

- **Photography:** Clean, professional, action-oriented
- **Athlete Imagery:** Dynamic shots showing movement and performance (with proper permissions)
- **Treatment:** Slight desaturation with color pop on greens/blues
- **Avoid:** Generic stock photos of people in clinics
- **Icons:** Line icons, consistent stroke weight, teal/navy color

### Overall Vibe

- **Premium but Approachable:** High-end feel without being intimidating
- **Clean & Modern:** Ample white space, clear hierarchy
- **Trustworthy:** Professional without being clinical/cold
- **Dynamic:** Suggests movement, performance, energy
