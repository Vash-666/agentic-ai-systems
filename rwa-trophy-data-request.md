## 2. ARTIFACT STORY & PROVENANCE

### What I Need

#### Origin Story
- **How did you acquire this trophy?** (inheritance, purchase, discovery, gift)
- **When did you acquire it?** (approximate year)
- **From whom did you acquire it?** (family member, estate sale, auction, etc.)
- **What was the context of the acquisition?** (cleaning out attic, intentional purchase, surprise find)

#### Physical Description
- **Dimensions:** Height x Width x Depth (in inches or cm)
- **Weight:** Approximate (if you've held it)
- **Wood type:** If known (oak, mahogany, walnut, etc.)
- **Plaque material:** Sterling silver? Silver-plated? Other metal?
- **Condition:** Rate 1-10, describe any damage, tarnish, repairs
- **Unique features:** Carvings, engravings, maker's marks, hallmarks
- **Backing/Hanging mechanism:** How is it meant to be displayed?

#### Historical Context
- **What tournament/league was this?** (club championship, regional, national?)
- **What location/city/region?**
- **Why 1945-1973 specifically?** (tournament founded in '45? ended in '73?)
- **Any notable champions?** (famous players, local legends, recurring winners)
- **What happened to the tournament?** (still running? why did it end?)

#### Documentation You Have
- **Photos:** Do you have photos of the trophy? (front, back, details, plaques)
- **Paperwork:** Certificates, receipts, letters, newspaper clippings?
- **Research:** Have you looked up any champions? Found any records?
- **Expert opinions:** Has anyone authenticated or appraised it?

### Why It Matters
- **Provenance = value:** The story behind an artifact can be worth more than the object itself
- **Buyer confidence:** Serious collectors want documented history
- **Marketing narrative:** A compelling story sells the NFT and the physical trophy
- **Historical preservation:** This is a piece of tennis history that deserves recording

### Format
Free-form paragraphs are fine. Write as much or as little as you know. Even fragments help—I can research gaps.
